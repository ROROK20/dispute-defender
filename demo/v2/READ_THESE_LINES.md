# Voice memo lines: Dispute Defender demo

18 short lines, about 2 minutes of talking in total. The video stretches each scene to fit your read, so take your time.

## How to record

- Find a quiet room. Hold the phone about a hand's width from your mouth.
- Read calmly, like you're explaining it to a friend. Don't rush.
- **Best:** one Voice Memo per line, renamed to its code (`A1`, `O1`, `O2` …).
- **Also fine:** one long memo with all 18 lines in order, with a **3-second pause** between lines. If you stumble, pause 3 seconds and read the whole line again. Then tell me which lines you re-read.
- **Send:** in Voice Memos, tap Share, then AirDrop to this Mac. The files land in `~/Downloads`. Then tell me "voice is in".

Say "Claude" like *clawed*.
