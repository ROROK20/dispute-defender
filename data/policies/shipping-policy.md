# Shipping Policy

Juniper Thread Co. (fictional sandbox store)
Published: 2026-01-15

## 1. Processing time

Orders ship within 2 business days of payment from our studio in Asheville, NC.

## 2. Carriers and tracking

All orders ship with USPS Ground Advantage or Priority Mail and include a tracking number. We add the tracking number to your PayPal transaction when the label is created.

## 3. Delivery estimates

Domestic delivery usually takes 3 to 6 business days after the item ships. Estimates are not guarantees; carrier delays are outside our control.

## 4. Signature confirmation

Orders of $100.00 or more ship with USPS Signature Confirmation.

## 5. Shipping address

We ship only to the address on your PayPal order. Please make sure it is correct before paying; we cannot redirect parcels once they ship.

## 6. Lost or missing parcels

If tracking shows a parcel as delivered and you cannot find it, check with neighbors and your local post office, then contact us within 14 days of the delivery date so we can open a carrier claim.
