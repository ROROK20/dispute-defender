# Returns & Refunds Policy

Juniper Thread Co. (fictional sandbox store)
Published: 2026-01-15

## 1. Return window

You may return any item within 30 days of the delivery date shown by the carrier.

## 2. Condition of returned items

Returned items must be unworn, unwashed and have the original tags attached. Bandanas, beanies and sleepwear are final sale once the hygiene seal is opened.

## 3. Fit and size exchanges

If an item does not fit, we will exchange it for another size at no charge, subject to stock. Please measure against our Size Guide before ordering; each listing names the size chart version it was cut to.

## 4. Items not as described

If an item arrives damaged, defective or materially different from its listing, contact us within 30 days of delivery. We will send a prepaid return label and refund the full purchase price, including original shipping.

## 5. Color and natural variation

Our garments are dyed and printed in small batches. Slight variation in shade between the listing photo and the item you receive is expected and is noted on the affected listings.

## 6. Refund timing

Refunds are issued to the original PayPal payment method within 5 business days of the returned item arriving at our studio.

## 7. Contact first

Please message us before opening a dispute. We reply to every message within 48 hours.
