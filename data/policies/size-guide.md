# Size Guide

Juniper Thread Co. (fictional sandbox store)
Published: 2026-01-15
Size chart version: v1

## 1. How to measure

Measure your body, not a garment. Chest: around the fullest part. Waist: around the narrowest part. Hips: around the fullest part, feet together.

## 2. Women's tops and dresses (chart v1, inches)

| Size | Chest | Waist | Hips |
|------|-------|-------|------|
| XS | 31-32 | 24-25 | 34-35 |
| S | 33-34 | 26-27 | 36-37 |
| M | 35-36 | 28-29 | 38-39 |
| L | 37-39 | 30-32 | 40-42 |
| XL | 40-42 | 33-35 | 43-45 |

## 5. Linen garments

Linen is cut to the chart above and relaxes slightly with wear. Wrap styles are cut to the waist measurement; if you are between sizes, choose the larger size.
